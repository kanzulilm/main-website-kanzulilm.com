What You Will Learn

Complete Persian Farsi Course Syllabus.

Arabic Alphabet & Reading

All 28 Arabic letters with correct pronunciation, connecting rules, vowel marks (harakat), and fluent reading of unvowelled Arabic text. Foundation level for Muslim kids and absolute beginners.

Arabic Writing

Arabic handwriting — all letter forms (initial, medial, final, isolated), word formation, and basic sentence writing. Essential for students who want full literacy in the Arabic language.

Quranic Vocabulary

The 1,500 most frequently used words in the Quran — covering approximately 80% of the Quran's text. Mastering these words allows Muslim children and adults to understand the Quran directly.

Sarf — Arabic Morphology

The science of Arabic word forms — root patterns (awzan), verb conjugations, and noun derivations. Sarf gives students the ability to recognize any Arabic word's meaning and grammatical category from its root.

Nahw — Arabic Grammar

Arabic sentence structure, subject-predicate relationships, case endings (I'rab), particles, and syntactic analysis. Nahw is the key to reading any classical Arabic text including the Quran and Hadith.

Conversational Arabic

Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha) speaking and comprehension — greetings, daily conversations, Islamic expressions, and structured dialogues for students in UK, USA, Canada, and Australia who want spoken Arabic fluency.
