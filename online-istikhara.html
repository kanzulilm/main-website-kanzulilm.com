What is Istikhara —
The Islamic Way to Decide.

Istikhara (الاستخارة) means seeking what is good. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "When any one of you deliberates about a decision, he should perform two rak'ahs of voluntary prayer and then say the Istikhara dua." (Bukhari). It opens the heart to Allah's ﷺ guidance and brings clarity through peace of mind and circumstances.

Istikhara does not necessarily produce a dream or sign. Our scholars explain the correct Istikhara method, perform the scholarly consultation on your behalf, and guide you on interpreting the outcome according to authentic Islamic principles.

At Kanzulilm International, our online Istikhara service is conducted by scholars trained in Islamic jurisprudence — supervised by Mufti Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari. Authentic, confidential, and serving Muslim families across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, and worldwide via WhatsApp.
