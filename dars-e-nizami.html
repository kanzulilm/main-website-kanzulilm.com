What is Dars-e-Nizami?

Dars-e-Nizami is the classical Islamic seminary curriculum developed by Mulla Nizamuddin Sihalivi (d. 1748 CE) from Firangi Mahal, near Lucknow, India. For over 300 years, this curriculum has been the standard of Islamic scholarship across South Asia and is now followed by thousands of madrasas worldwide — including in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, and South Africa.

The curriculum begins with intensive Arabic language training — Sarf (morphology) and Nahw (grammar) — giving the student the linguistic tools to read original classical texts. It then advances through the core Islamic sciences: Tafseer, Hadith, Fiqh, Usul al-Fiqh, and Aqaid — culminating in the Dawra-e-Hadith: the study of all six major Hadith collections (Sihah Sittah).

At Kanzulilm International, our online Dars-e-Nizami course follows this same authentic curriculum under PhD-level scholars — entirely via WhatsApp, from anywhere in the world, without leaving your home, job, or family.

Curriculum

12 Classical Islamic Sciences Covered.

Sarf — Arabic Morphology

The science of Arabic word formation — root patterns, verb conjugations, and derivational structures. Without Sarf, reading unvowelled classical Arabic texts is impossible. The foundational first subject of the Dars-e-Nizami curriculum.

Nahw — Arabic Grammar

Arabic sentence structure, I'rab (case endings), and syntactic analysis. Nahw is the grammatical key that unlocks the meaning of the Quran, Hadith, and all classical Islamic literature — studied across multiple years of the curriculum.

Balagha — Arabic Rhetoric

The science of eloquence and literary beauty in Arabic. Essential for understanding the miraculous linguistic nature of the Quran and the eloquence of Prophetic Hadith — studied at intermediate-advanced level in the programme.

Tafseer & Ulum al-Quran

Classical Quranic exegesis — context of revelation (Shan-e-Nuzool), abrogation (Nasikh wa Mansukh), and the legal and spiritual rulings extracted from each Surah by the major classical commentators.

Hadith & Mustalah al-Hadith

The science of Prophetic narrations — chain of transmission (Isnad), narrator criticism (Rijal), classification by authenticity, and the principles of Hadith science. Essential for independent Islamic scholarship.

Sihah Sittah — Six Hadith Books

The six major Hadith collections in the Dawra-e-Hadith year: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawood, Jami' al-Tirmidhi, Sunan al-Nasai, and Sunan Ibn Majah — the pinnacle of the Dars-e-Nizami curriculum.

Fiqh — Islamic Jurisprudence

Hanafi Islamic law covering all areas of practical life — prayer, fasting, Zakat, Hajj, marriage, divorce, business transactions, and inheritance. Studied through classical texts including Mukhtasar al-Quduri and Hidaya.

Usul al-Fiqh — Principles of Law

The theoretical framework of Islamic law — how rulings are derived from the Quran, Sunnah, Ijma, and Qiyas. Usul al-Fiqh trains the student to think like a Faqih and understand the methodology behind every ruling.

Aqaid — Islamic Theology

The science of Islamic belief — the attributes of Allah, Prophethood, the Hereafter, and scholastic theology (Kalam). Aqaid grounds the student in the correct beliefs of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah.
