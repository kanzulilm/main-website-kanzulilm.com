Why Every Muslim Needs Seerah

Seerah is Not Just History —
It is a Living Guide.

Allah ﷺ says in the Quran: "Indeed in the Messenger of Allah you have the finest example to follow." (Surah Al-Ahzab 33:21). The Seerah is not simply Islamic history — it is the practical demonstration of how to live Islam. Every aspect of the Prophet's ﷺ life — his patience in Makkah, his leadership in Madinah, his generosity, his mercy, his courage — is a direct lesson for Muslim men, women, and Muslim children today.

Muslim kids and young adults in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, and Australia grow up surrounded by other cultures and role models. The Seerah gives them the most complete and inspiring role model who ever lived — studied not as a distant figure from the past, but as someone whose decisions and character directly apply to the challenges of daily life.

At Kanzulilm International, the online Seerah course is taught from the authentic classical sources — narrated by scholars who bring each event to life, connect it to Quranic verses and Hadith, draw out the lessons, and make the Prophet ﷺ real and beloved in the hearts of students.

SEERAH TIMELINE — FROM BIRTH TO PASSING

570 CE

Birth in Makkah

Born in the Year of the Elephant. Orphaned early — raised by grandfather Abd al-Muttalib, then uncle Abu Talib

610 CE

First Revelation

Jibreel ﷺ descends in Cave Hira. "Iqra" — the first word of the Quran. Khadijah RA becomes first Muslim

615 CE

Migration to Abyssinia

First Hijrah — Muslims flee persecution to Christian King Negus. Quraish's torture intensifies

619 CE

Year of Sorrow (Am al-Huzn)

Passing of Khadijah RA and Abu Talib. Isra wal Miraj — Night Journey and Ascension to Heavens

622 CE

Hijrah to Madinah

The pivotal migration. Masjid al-Nabawi built. Charter of Madinah. Islamic state established

624 CE

Battle of Badr

313 Muslims defeat 1,000 Quraish. First major victory of Islam. Divine intervention confirmed

630 CE

Conquest of Makkah

Fatah Makkah — 10,000 Muslims enter Makkah. General amnesty. Ka'bah purified of idols

632 CE

Farewell & Passing

Hajjat ul Wada — 100,000 companions. Final sermon. Passing of the Prophet ﷺ aged 63

What You Will Study

Complete Seerah Course Syllabus.

Early Life & Pre-Prophethood

The Prophet's ﷺ birth in the Year of the Elephant, his early orphanhood, upbringing by Abdul Muttalib and Abu Talib, his journeys to Syria, his marriage to Khadijah RA, and his character before Prophethood — Al-Amin (The Trustworthy). Understanding who he was before revelation is foundational to loving who he became.

First Revelation & Early Islam

The night in Cave Hira, the descent of Jibreel ﷺ, the first words "Iqra bismi rabbik" — the first verse of the Quran. The early converts: Khadijah RA, Abu Bakr RA, Ali RA, Uthman RA, Zaid bin Harithah RA. The secret years of dawah and the first open call on Mount Safa.

Persecution in Makkah

The torture of Bilal RA, Sumayyah RA (the first martyr of Islam), Ammar RA, and the weak Muslims. The economic boycott of Banu Hashim for 3 years. The Year of Sorrow — passing of Khadijah RA and Abu Talib. Isra wal Miraj — the Night Journey to Jerusalem and ascension through the Heavens.

Hijrah & Building Madinah

The pivotal migration to Madinah in 622 CE — marking the Islamic calendar. The building of Masjid al-Nabawi. The Charter of Madinah — the world's first written constitution. The brotherhood (Muakhaat) between Muhajireen and Ansar. How the Prophet ﷺ built a new community from nothing.

Major Battles & Expeditions

Badr (624 CE) — 313 vs 1,000. Uhud (625 CE) — the lesson of obedience. Khandaq / Ahzab (627 CE) — the Battle of the Trench. Khaybar (628 CE). Hunayn (630 CE). Each battle studied with its causes, events, outcomes, and the timeless lessons the Prophet ﷺ taught through them.

Conquest of Makkah & Final Years

The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah and its wisdom. Fatah Makkah (630 CE) — 10,000 Muslims, general amnesty for enemies. Purification of the Ka'bah. Hajjat ul Wada — the Farewell Pilgrimage and the last sermon. The final illness and passing of the Prophet ﷺ at 63 — and the world's grief.

Character & Daily Life of the Prophet ﷺ

His physical description (Hilyah). His mercy to enemies. His love for children. His humility in the home. His generosity and simplicity. His relationships with his wives, Companions, and non-Muslims. The Shama'il — the detailed accounts of his character narrated by those who lived with him ﷺ.

The Companions (Sahaba RA)

The lives of the major Companions who shaped the Seerah — Abu Bakr RA, Umar RA, Uthman RA, Ali RA, Khadijah RA, Aisha RA, Fatimah RA, Bilal RA, Khalid bin Walid RA — and how their stories, sacrifices, and transformations illuminate the greatness of the Prophet ﷺ.

Classical Seerah Sources

Two primary texts: Ibn Hisham's Seerah — the oldest and most authoritative classical biography. Al-Bidayah wal-Nihayah by Ibn Katheer — encyclopedic and Hadith-verified.
