Teachers, Credentials & Ijazah

Yes — without exception. Our faculty comprises certified Huffaz ul Quran, certified Qurra (Tajweed specialists with verified Ijazah chains), Dars-e-Nizami graduates, qualified Muftis with Takhassus fil Fiqh, M.Phil researchers, and PhD scholars in Islamic sciences — all holding credentials from traditional accredited Islamic scholarly authorities. No unverified tutors are ever appointed at Kanzulilm International.
An Ijazah is a formal scholarly authorization — a documented, unbroken chain of transmission linking a teacher's knowledge back through generations of scholars to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ himself. For Quran and Tajweed teaching, a verified Ijazah is the gold standard of authenticity — it means your child is not learning from a YouTube video or an uncertified tutor, but from a scholar whose knowledge carries a 1,400-year living chain of transmission. At Kanzulilm, an Ijazah chain is a mandatory requirement for every Quran and Tajweed teacher.
The Dars-e-Nizami (full Alim programme) and advanced Fiqh courses are taught under the direct supervision of Mufti Pir Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari — who holds the Dars-e-Nizami twice (from two separate traditional accredited Islamic scholarly authorities) and the Takhassus fil Fiqh (postgraduate Mufti qualification). For specific subject modules, other qualified Dars-e-Nizami graduates and Muftis from our verified faculty are also assigned as appropriate.

Istikhara & Rohani Ilaj

Istikhara guidance is available for marriage decisions, business matters, health choices, and major life decisions — strictly in accordance with the Quran and Sunnah, with complete confidentiality.
Rohani Ilaj (Islamic spiritual healing) refers to the use of authenticated Quranic Wazaif, supplications, and spiritually prescribed remedies drawn from the Quran and Prophetic tradition to address health challenges, emotional difficulties, and personal hardships. At Kanzulilm, this service is provided by Mufti Pir Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari who holds a comprehensive Ijazat-e-Amma from senior Mashaikh — meaning every remedy prescribed carries authenticated scholarly authorization. All Rohani Ilaj is conducted strictly within the boundaries of the Quran and Sunnah.
