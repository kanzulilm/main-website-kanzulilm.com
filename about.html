About Kanzulilm International.

Founded in 2009 under the scholarly authority of Mufti Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari, Kanzulilm International — whose name means Treasury of Knowledge — was established with a single purpose: to make authentic certified Islamic education accessible to every Muslim, wherever in the world they may be.

What began as a personal commitment to preserving the classical sciences of Islam has grown into a trusted online Islamic academy serving students across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, and beyond — all through personal 1-on-1 live sessions that preserve the traditional teacher-student bond.

Founder & Director — Kanzulilm International

Hafiz ul Quran Qari Mufti Alim Spiritual Guide

By the grace of Almighty Allah and in devoted service to the Quran and Sunnah, Mufti Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari founded Kanzulilm International in 2009 — a scholar whose entire life has been consecrated to the propagation of Prophetic sciences, the service of humanity, and the spiritual reformation of the Muslim Ummah.

He is a formally certified Hafiz ul Quran, having memorized the complete Holy Quran under rigorous scholarly supervision and received official recognition from traditional accredited Islamic boards. He further completed the classical science of Tajweed and Qiraat and qualified as a certified Qari — trained to teach every letter from its precise Makhraj with its correct Sifat. Beyond the academic distinction, Allah Almighty has blessed him with an exceptionally melodious and soul-stirring voice in Quranic recitation, such that all who hear him are moved to admiration without exception.

He completed the full Dars-e-Nizami curriculum not once, but twice — receiving formal degrees from two independent traditional accredited Islamic scholarly authorities. He then completed the advanced Takhassus fil Fiqh (Mufti Course) under verified scholarly supervision — conferring upon him the distinguished title of Mufti, with full authority to deliberate and issue Fatwas in the light of the Quran and Sunnah.

Eminent Ulama and Mashaikh from across the Muslim world have granted him personal Ijazah in the narration of Hadith through verified scholarly chains — a living connection to the Prophetic tradition. He holds full authorization to accept Bay'ah across all major Silasil al-Tasawwuf, and a comprehensive Ijazat-e-Amma in all recognized Wazaif and Amaliyat — transmitted through verified scholarly chains and traditional authorities whose lineages trace back to the earliest generations of Islam.

Imam, Khatib & Spiritual Guide

He fulfills the distinguished responsibilities of Imam and Khatib in mosques with consummate excellence. His lectures, sermons, and scholarly discourses are so deeply affecting and spiritually resonant that listeners from across the world report profound transformation upon hearing them. Alongside his mastery of the outward sciences, he dedicates himself to the spiritual guidance and moral reformation of the Ummah — serving as a trusted channel for resolving spiritual difficulties through authentic Rohani Ilaj and Islamic spiritual counseling, strictly within the framework of the Quran and Prophetic Sunnah.

He also holds multiple certifications in Information Technology from recognized institutions — enabling Kanzulilm International to deliver classical Islamic education through the most reliable modern platforms, combining scholarly depth with digital accessibility.

Students, Institutions & The Vision of 2009

His circle of graduates is remarkably distinguished — encompassing Huffaz ul Quran, certified Qurra, Dars-e-Nizami graduates, qualified Muftis, and accomplished professionals including PhD scholars, medical doctors, engineers, university professors, and academic researchers. These students serve with sincerity across Pakistan and in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, and the Middle East. Numerous Islamic institutions, madrasas, and religious organizations operate under his formal patronage and scholarly supervision.

When he founded Kanzulilm International in 2009, his intention was clear: authentic Islamic knowledge must not be limited by geography. That pioneering vision has been validated by time — over 500 students across four continents have experienced what Kanzulilm truly is: not a platform, but a personal relationship between a qualified scholar and a sincere student, conducted one lesson at a time.

Our Journey

The Story of Kanzulilm.

Before 2009 — The Foundation of Learning

Years of Academic Dedication

Mufti Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari spent years in formal study — memorizing the Quran, mastering Tajweed and Qiraat, completing the Dars-e-Nizami not once but twice across two separate boards, and then specializing in Fiqh. This was not a quick path. It was a life built on the love of knowledge.

2009 — The Founding

Kanzulilm International is Established

With a clear vision and a deep sense of responsibility, Kanzulilm International was founded. The name — meaning Treasury of Knowledge — reflected the ambition: not just to teach, but to preserve and transmit the complete treasury of classical Islamic sciences to a new generation of Muslims worldwide.

2009–2015 — Building the Curriculum

Expanding Beyond Borders

The early years were spent building a rigorous, structured curriculum that preserved the depth of classical scholarship while being accessible to students in the West. One-on-one live sessions over WhatsApp became the primary teaching method — allowing the personal teacher-student relationship that authentic Islamic education has always demanded.

2015–2020 — Growth & Global Reach

Serving Muslims Across Four Continents

Students from the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, Europe, and the Middle East began enrolling in increasing numbers. The word spread the traditional way — through families who experienced the quality of teaching and shared it with others. No paid advertising. Simply the reputation of genuine scholarship.

2020–Present — The Digital Academy

A Complete Islamic Education Platform

With a fully developed website, structured course pages, Islamic audio resources, and digital academic tools including the Kanz ul Ilm Quran Navigator and Word-by-Word Morphology platform, Kanzulilm International today stands as a complete online Islamic education ecosystem — rooted in 15 years of proven scholarly tradition.

What We Stand For

Our Principles &
Academic Values.

Authenticity Above All

We teach only what has been verified through certified scholarly chains. No self-taught instructors. No YouTube-based certificates. Every course reflects the classical tradition as transmitted through legitimate academic boards.

The 1-on-1 Commitment

Group classes may be efficient. They are not effective for Islamic education. Every single student at Kanzulilm receives the undivided personal attention of a qualified scholar — in the same way knowledge was always transmitted, from one heart to another.

Accessibility Without Compromise

Making authentic knowledge accessible to every Muslim, everywhere — this is the founding promise of Kanzulilm. Accessibility does not mean lowering standards. It means ensuring that the standards of classical scholarship reach every student, regardless of geography.

Respect for the Student

Every student — whether a six-year-old learning Qaida or a middle-aged professional seeking to correct their Quran recitation — is treated with patience, encouragement, and respect. The love of learning flourishes only in an environment of genuine care.

Scholarly Humility

True scholars are the first to acknowledge the vastness of what they do not yet know. Mufti Syed Muhammad Usman Bukhari approaches teaching not as a performance of authority but as an act of service — sharing what was given to him so that others may benefit and the tradition may live on.

Technology in Service of Tradition

We do not believe technology replaces the scholar-student relationship. We believe it removes the geographic barrier that once prevented that relationship from forming. WhatsApp, online platforms, and digital tools are our means — the classical sciences are our purpose.
