Understanding the Service

What is Rohani Ilaj —
The Quranic Path to Healing.

Rohani Ilaj (روحانی علاج) — Spiritual Treatment — is the Islamic practice of seeking healing through Quranic recitation, prophetic duas, prescribed Wazaif, and Ruqyah Shariah. Allah ﷺ says: "We send down from the Quran that which is a healing and mercy for believers." (17:82). The Quran is complete healing (Shifa) for the heart, soul, and body.

Ruqyah Shariah is the Prophetic method of healing using specific Quranic verses — Surah Al-Fatiha, Ayat ul Kursi, Surah Al-Falaq, and Surah An-Nas. It is the authentic Islamic response to Nazar (evil eye), Sihr (black magic), jinn affliction, spiritual blockages, emotional distress, and physical illness where Allah's ﷺ blessing is sought.

At Kanzulilm International, our Rohani Ilaj service is conducted strictly according to the Quran and authentic Sunnah — not cultural practices or unauthenticated methods. Our scholars diagnose each case individually, prescribe correct Quranic Wazaif, guide the method of recitation, and provide ongoing WhatsApp follow-up for Muslim families worldwide.

How We Help

Our Rohani Ilaj Consultation Process.

Step 1: Confidential WhatsApp Consultation

Share your situation privately with our scholar via WhatsApp. Describe your symptoms, concerns, and experiences. All information is strictly confidential — bound by Islamic Amanah. Nothing is shared with anyone.

Step 2: Scholarly Assessment

Our Islamic scholar assesses your case in light of the Quran and authentic Sunnah. The scholar determines whether the situation involves Nazar, Sihr, spiritual distress, emotional factors, or a combination — and identifies the correct Quranic treatment pathway.

Step 3: Personalised Quranic Wazaif

The scholar prescribes specific Quranic verses, authentic duas from Sahih Bukhari and Muslim, and daily Wazaif appropriate for your case — with instructions on method, timing, quantity, and duration of recitation.

Step 4: Ruqyah Shariah Guidance

Where Ruqyah is indicated, the scholar provides the exact Quranic verses from the authentic Sunnah, the method of self-Ruqyah, and guidance on protective measures and daily Adhkar for ongoing spiritual protection.

Step 5: Ongoing Follow-Up

Kanzulilm provides ongoing WhatsApp follow-up throughout the healing process. The scholar monitors progress, adjusts prescriptions as needed, and continues guidance until the situation is resolved.
